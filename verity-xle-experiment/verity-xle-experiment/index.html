This is an experimental basket proxy, not NAV, IIV, a recommendation, or an exchange-calculated fair value. Because it tracks only the ten largest holdings and ignores cash, fees, creation/redemption effects and the remaining portfolio, some divergence is expected. The point of the test is to measure that divergence.
